Project lead

DigiHip

Digital perioperative care platform · Web + Mobile

Started as an undergraduate thesis project at CBML, FORTH, and grew into a full clinical deployment built with orthopedic clinicians at Venizeleio Hospital, Crete — a doctor portal and a companion patient app that keep total hip arthroplasty patients on track from surgery through recovery.

  • Doctor Portal — registration, scheduling, questionnaire dashboard, recovery reports, secure messaging
  • Patient App — onboarding, daily check-ins, recovery tracking, doctor–patient chat
